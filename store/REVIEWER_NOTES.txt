The extension has no account, backend, external API, remote code, analytics, or network requests.

To test:
1. Open any ordinary webpage.
2. Select at least 20 characters of visible text.
3. Click the extension toolbar icon to open the side panel.
4. Click the button labeled “读取选中文字”.
5. Enter a goal and success criterion.
6. Click “分析偏航”.
7. The extension performs deterministic local analysis and displays a corrective prompt.
8. Test export, checkpoint and delete-all functions.

The activeTab/scripting permissions are used only to read window.getSelection().toString() after a user gesture. No unselected page content is read.
